# Provenance

Based on [MengWoods/video-privacy-blur](https://github.com/MengWoods/video-privacy-blur), commit `9dea3f0ea4789f6b28e462be69bddeae14754b7c`.

The upstream copyright notice and MIT license are retained in `LICENSE`. This version adds resource-aware parallel batch processing, resumable jobs, optional temporal gap filling, and additional obscuring methods. The default Gaussian blur divisor is 1. EgoBlur-style filtering follows the large-kernel mean-filter approach used by [EgoBlur](https://github.com/facebookresearch/EgoBlur). Third-party dependencies and model weights are distributed under their respective licenses. The bundled `models/yolov8n-face.pt` was downloaded from the [YapaLab/yolo-face release](https://github.com/YapaLab/yolo-face/releases/tag/1.0.0); its source URL, file size, and locally computed SHA-256 are recorded in `models/yolov8n-face.pt.source.json`. The checksum has not been verified against an official published digest. Other model weights and third-party dependencies are not included.
